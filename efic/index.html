EFIC European Diploma in Pain Physiotherapy — Study Hub & Exam Roadmap

Target Exam Date: November 13th
Format: Part 1: Multiple Choice Examination (MCQ) followed by Part 2: Practical Clinical Examination (communication, clinical examination, diagnosis, clinical reasoning).
Primary Source Base: European Pain Federation (EFIC®) Core Curriculum & Recommended Reading List (2017).

🎯 Exam Strategy & Structure

The EFIC MCQ examination questions are drawn directly from the Core Curriculum and its Recommended Reading List. The reading list comprises seminal systematic reviews, Cochrane overviews, clinical classification papers, and biopsychosocial management models across four core domains:

EFIC Core Curriculum (4 Domains)
 ├── Section 1: Pain Science & Knowledge (Taxonomy, Mechanisms, Neurobiology, Neuroplasticity)
 ├── Section 2: Principles of Assessment & Measurement (ICF, Neuropathic Grading, Mechanisms Classification, Psychometrics)
 ├── Section 3: Principles of Treatment (Communication, Education/PNE, Behavioral, Exercise, Multidisciplinary, Work, Modalities, CAM)
 └── Section 4: Pain Subgroups & Special Populations (CRPS, Dementia, Geriatrics, Paediatrics & Adolescents, RTS/SIS)

🗓️ Study Timeline: Target Nov 13th

  1. Phase 1: Core Synthesis Mastery (Weeks 1–4)
    • Master Sections 1 & 2 (Taxonomy, Nociceptive/Neuropathic/Central Sensitization definitions, Finnerup grading, Brain networks).
    • Review high-yield mechanism-based classifications.
  2. Phase 2: Treatment & Interventions Evidence (Weeks 5–7)
    • Master Cochrane review conclusions: Exercise in fibromyalgia (Bidonde), Graded Exposure vs Activity (Macedo), PNE (Geneen, Moseley), TENS in phantom pain (Johnson), Ultrasound in LBP (Ebadi — ineffective), SMT (Rubinstein).
  3. Phase 3: Special Populations & Subgroups (Weeks 8–9)
    • CRPS PT hierarchy (Smart 2016), Paediatric pain tools (Hamill, Hatfield), Elderly & Dementia observational tools (Lichtner, Herr).
  4. Phase 4: EFIC Academy & Practice Exam Simulation (Weeks 10–Final)
    • Incorporate EFIC Academy modules and video lectures as they become active.
    • Run simulated MCQ blocks and timed drill questions.
